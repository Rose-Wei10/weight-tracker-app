# Privacy policy — draft for review

This is an unpublished draft, not legal advice or a final policy. Before beta, identify the legal operator, public contact/support address, jurisdictions, minimum age, hosting region, retention periods and effective date. Publish this policy and link it in the app before account creation.

Forma is intended for adults 18 and older. It stores an email/account identifier, user-entered weight/date/waist records, food/portion/calorie/macronutrient records, goal/profile estimates, language/units, and optional AI sharing consent using Firebase services. Height, age and the selected formula coefficient support the optional goal calculator. The app does not connect to HealthKit, Health Connect, steps, location or advertising networks.

Camera access is requested when taking a meal photo. Photo selection uses the platform picker. Only the photo you select and explicitly submit goes to AI. Local images are compressed to JPEG without EXIF; temporary files may remain in OS-managed cache. Originals remain in your own library. We do not save meal photographs to our database.

When you opt in and request AI support, your submitted text/photo and bounded recent chat context are sent through our server to OpenRouter and an eligible model provider. Weekly insight requests send aggregated logged-day counts and averages. Request flags restrict routes to non-training, zero-retention endpoints, but the final provider's policy and contractual terms must be confirmed before launch. Do not submit faces, documents or identifying details. Turning sharing off prevents future requests; it cannot undo a previous transfer. The app does not persist conversations. A confirmed AI food becomes part of your diary and is labeled approximate.

Firebase and providers may process service metadata and security logs. No third-party advertising, behavioral analytics or crash reporting SDK is configured by this branch. The production operator must specify Firebase log/back-up retention, provider subprocessors, regional storage and applicable international-transfer safeguards.

You can edit/delete individual logs, export CSV or full JSON, and initiate account/data deletion in Profile after password verification. Deletion removes active profile/subcollections, templates, AI quota records and the Auth account. A temporary UID tombstone blocks stale tokens and is scheduled to expire after two hours (TTL processing can be delayed). Infrastructure logs, backups and already-transmitted provider data follow separately disclosed retention terms. The public deletion/support process and exact retention periods must be finalized before launch.

Nutrition and weight data are private to the authenticated account under the provided rules. No system is perfectly secure; report issues through the final published support channel. The operator must state applicable access/correction/deletion/complaint rights and any required response periods after legal review.
